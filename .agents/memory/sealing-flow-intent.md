---
name: Simple document sealing
description: User's intended Android workflow and why separate scan and seal entry points are unsuitable.
---

The user wants Android to be as simple as the website: "just seal the document
or seal the document with a forensic report." They objected to the separate
starting scan flow and to the forensic option under sealing only returning a
sealed document.

**Why:** The user said the app was "trying to do too much" and the same intended
operation behaved differently depending on where they started.

**How to apply:** Treat analysis as the explicit optional addition to sealing,
not as another primary journey. Preserve available GPS capture and saved
evidence; do not interpret simplification as permission to remove legal chat,
delete case history, or add website-style cloud uploads.
